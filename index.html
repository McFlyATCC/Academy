Welcome

The most important thing I can tell you up front: don't be afraid to build things and test them. That goes double for new technology that throws out the way we've always done it. Reading about it will only take you so far. Get your hands on it, push it until something breaks, and then work out why it broke.

The Day Job

When I'm not teaching, I'm working to secure Best Buy's networks against attack. Five-plus years in, I've specialized in SIEM, automation and AI, and cloud security.

That work is a big part of why this course looks the way it does. The tooling and the threats both move faster than any textbook can keep up with, so what I bring into class is what's actually landing in production right now.

How I Got Here

IT was a mid-life career switch. I came out of the medical field and went back to a two-year AAS program at Lake Superior College in Duluth. I had always been the family virus cleaner, so I figured I'd try a career where the stakes weren't life or death.

Cybersecurity found me there. Vickie, a former ATCC and LSC instructor and CCDC coach, promised us a trip to Chicago if we won the State CCDC competition. We won, and regionals in Chicago was a blast. Thanks, Vickie.

I competed the maximum five years and took state in four of them. I've been coaching for a decade since, and I co-coach the ATCC CCDC team with Steven Van Voorhis.

Teaching is how I give back to the future of this field. Passionate students are my favorite part of the job — if you're the one asking the extra question, you're the reason I keep showing up.

Course Tools

I build small interactive tools to practice the things that are hard to learn from a slide. They run in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to sign into.

  • Linux Command Trainer Files, permissions, users, processes.
  • Hash Lab Hashing vs. encoding, password strength.

One Last Thing

Find your passion. IT is a huge field with more specialties than you can count, and you are not required to love all of them. Find the part that makes you want to keep reading after the assignment is done, and you'll go far in this career.

Just remember me when you get there — and if you're ever in a position to, donate to the ATCC CCDC team.

Build it. Test it. Break it. Find out why. The tech that scares people is usually the tech worth learning.
