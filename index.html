Welcome

The most important thing I can tell you up front: don't be afraid to build things and test them. That goes double for new technology that throws out the way we've always done it. Reading about it will only take you so far. Get your hands on it, push it until something breaks, and then work out why it broke.

Course Tools

I build small interactive tools to practice the things that are hard to learn from a slide. They run in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to sign into.

  • Linux Command Trainer Files, permissions, users, processes.
  • Hash Lab Hashing vs. encoding, password strength.
  • CIA Triad Trainer Classify incidents by the pillar they break.
  • VM Console VM lifecycle and the Shared Responsibility Model.

One Last Thing

Find your passion. IT is a huge field with more specialties than you can count, and you are not required to love all of them. Find the part that makes you want to keep reading after the assignment is done, and you'll go far in this career.

Just remember me when you get there — and if you're ever in a position to, donate to the ATCC CCDC team.

Build it. Test it. Break it. Find out why. The tech that scares people is usually the tech worth learning.
